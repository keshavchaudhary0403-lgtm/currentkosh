# Platform research: how aspirants prepare current affairs today (Oct 2026)

This is a summary of a desk study of Vision IAS, Drishti IAS, ForumIAS, InsightsIAS, Vajiram, StudyIQ, Testbook, Adda247, Oliveboard, AffairsCloud, GKToday, Jagran Josh, PRS, Unacademy, Mrunal, Sleepy Classes, and AI-first apps (SuperKalam, PadhAI, PrepAiro, RetainIt, SpacedRevision), plus YouTube formats.

**Caveat on the evidence:** Reddit and Play Store pages could not be fetched. The complaint evidence comes from G2, Trustpilot, Voxya, app-review analytics and comparison blogs, and several of those are vendor-written. Do a manual pass of r/UPSC and r/SSC before treating the ranking below as final.

## What aspirants complain about

1. **Too many overlapping sources.** The same news appears 3–8 times, and there is a "compilation duplication problem" across monthly magazines.
2. **Too long to finish.** Monthly magazines run 120–200 pages, and daily videos run about 60 minutes.
3. **Pop-ups and ads**, sometimes in the middle of a mock test (Adda247's most-reported issue).
4. **Sales calls, upsells and refund problems** (Testbook, Unacademy).
5. **Cluttered apps** that mix courses, jobs, tests and news.
6. **Weak Hindi:** machine-translated, late, and full of obscure terms.
7. **No source citations.** AI summaries don't link to their sources, and free GK compilations contain factual errors.
8. **No real revision loop.** "Revision" means re-reading a PDF, even though 67 of 100 Prelims 2025 questions were statement-based.
9. **No exam-specific filtering.** SSC/Banking aspirants and UPSC aspirants need different cuts of the same news, and state PSC current affairs are scattered.
10. **Stale content** when educators leave or apps stop updating.

## Patterns that work (copied into CurrentKosh)

- **A fixed daily amount** (Vajiram's 10 pointers, ForumIAS's 10 MCQs, Oliveboard's 10 cards). In CurrentKosh: *Today's brief* with a clear "done" state.
- **Day → week → month → year roll-ups** (Vision's chain up to PT365). In CurrentKosh: weekly and monthly recaps and tests built from one story base.
- **Separate Prelims and Mains layers.** In CurrentKosh: key facts plus MCQs, with a separate Mains angle.
- **Tagging by syllabus and GS paper**, and choosing news by PYQ relevance. In CurrentKosh: GS paper tags, static-GK links, and a relevance score per exam.
- **Grounding in primary sources** (PIB, PRS). In CurrentKosh: official sources only for AI notes, linked on every card.
- **Short recall drills plus long explainers.** In CurrentKosh: flashcards, quick checks and storylines.
- **Streaks, weak-area lists and analytics.** In CurrentKosh: Progress, plus weak-area drills.

## Gaps nobody fills well, and how CurrentKosh answers them

| Gap | CurrentKosh |
|---|---|
| One deduplicated, trusted feed | Triage merges reports of the same event across sources and languages |
| Spaced repetition for current affairs | Fact-level SRS: flashcards from what you read, and every question you got wrong |
| Following a story over time | Storylines: a timeline view and a storyline test |
| Citations on every fact | Official source links on every story, plus "Also covered by" |
| Same-day, native Hindi | EN and HI generated together and written natively, with a bilingual mode and glossary |
| One content base, sliced per exam | A 0–3 relevance score per exam, and MCQs in UPSC and SSC styles |
| State PSC current affairs | Stories tagged with state codes; the user's home state is boosted |
| Calm, ad-free experience | No ads or pop-ups; three primary entry points (Today / Revise / Practice) |
| "What can I skip?" | Relevance badges (Must-know / Likely / Optional) |

## Still to build (from the research)

- Mains answer evaluation (the most-praised AI feature among competitors).
- "Ask this story" Q&A, grounded only in that story's sources.
- An audio or 60-second version of the daily brief.
- An auto-built PT365-style yearly compilation per exam, exportable to PDF.
- Offline / low-data mode for low-end Android phones.
- A monthly coverage report: what share of the latest exam's current-affairs questions CurrentKosh's stories covered.
